Privacy Policy — Voltbox: Electrician Toolkit

Last updated: July 20, 2026

Voltbox: Electrician Toolkit ("Voltbox", "the App") is developed by UsableUtilities ("we", "us"). This policy explains what data the App does and does not collect.

Summary

Voltbox does not collect, store, or transmit any personal data. The App works entirely on your device, with no account, no server, and no analytics.

Data We Collect

None. Voltbox has no backend server and does not require account creation. All calculator inputs, saved calculations, projects, and preferences (theme, language, text size, and whether you've unlocked Pro) are stored locally on your device only, using standard iOS/Android local storage. We have no access to this data, and it is never transmitted to us or to any third party.

In-App Purchases

Voltbox offers an optional one-time "Pro" unlock. Purchases are processed entirely by Apple (App Store / StoreKit) or Google (Google Play Billing). We do not receive, process, or store your payment information — it is handled directly between you and Apple/Google under their own privacy policies:

  • Apple Privacy Policy
  • Google Privacy Policy

Third-Party Services

Voltbox does not integrate any third-party analytics, advertising, or tracking SDKs.

Children's Privacy

Voltbox does not knowingly collect data from anyone, including children, because it does not collect data at all.

Changes to This Policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted at this same address with a revised "Last updated" date.
