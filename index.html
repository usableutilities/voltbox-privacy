Privacy Policy — UsableUtilities

Last updated: August 6, 2026

This policy applies to every app published by UsableUtilities ("we", "us", "our apps"), including Voltbox: Electrician Toolkit, Voltbox: PV Installer Toolkit, Brickbox: Mason Toolkit, Vectorbox: Pilot Toolkit, Timberbox: Carpenter Toolkit, and any future app in our lineup. It explains what data our apps do and do not collect.

Summary

Our apps do not collect, store, or transmit any personal data. Every app we publish works entirely on your device, with no account and no analytics — this is a deliberate, company-wide design choice, not something that varies app to app. The one exception is described below: Vectorbox's optional weather lookup feature, which fetches public aviation weather data from a third-party government service.

Data We Collect

None. Our apps do not require account creation, and none of our apps operate a server or database of our own. Any inputs, saved data, and preferences (theme, language, text size, unlocked features, etc.) are stored locally on your device only, using standard iOS/Android local storage. We have no access to this data, and it is never transmitted to us or to any third party.

Weather Data (Vectorbox only)

Vectorbox: Pilot Toolkit includes an optional Weather Lookup feature (METAR/TAF) that sends the airport identifier you search for directly to the National Oceanic and Atmospheric Administration's public Aviation Weather Center API (aviationweather.gov), a free U.S. government service that requires no account or API key. No personal or device data is sent — only the airport code you enter. We do not operate this service, do not see or store your queries, and the request goes directly from your device to NOAA. The most recent successful result is cached locally on your device (not on any server of ours) so it remains available if you look it up again without a connection. Every other feature in Vectorbox, and every feature in every other app we publish, works fully offline with no network request of any kind.

In-App Purchases

Some of our apps offer an optional one-time "Pro" unlock. Purchases are processed entirely by Apple (App Store / StoreKit) or Google (Google Play Billing). We do not receive, process, or store your payment information — it is handled directly between you and Apple/Google under their own privacy policies:

  • Google Privacy Policy

Third-Party Services

Our apps do not integrate any third-party analytics, advertising, or tracking SDKs.

Children's Privacy

Our apps do not knowingly collect data from anyone, including children, because they do not collect data at all.
